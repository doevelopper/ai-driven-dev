# PI Planning

PI Planning is the heartbeat of the ART. It is a 2-day event held at the start of every Planning Interval where all teams align on a shared vision, plan their iterations, and commit to PI Objectives.

## PI Planning Agenda (Standard 2-Day Format)

### Day 1
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| Morning | Business Context — Business Owners present strategy & vision |
| Morning | Product/Solution Vision — Product Management presents roadmap |
| Afternoon | Architecture Vision — System Architect presents enablers |
| Afternoon | Team Breakouts Round 1 — Teams plan iterations 1–5 |
| End of Day | Draft PI Objectives — Teams present draft plans |

### Day 2
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| Morning | Team Breakouts Round 2 — Adjust plans based on Day 1 feedback |
| Afternoon | Final Plan Review — All teams present final PI Objectives |
| Afternoon | Risk Identification — ROAM risks (Resolved/Owned/Accepted/Mitigated) |
| Close | Management Review & Confidence Vote |

## Confidence Vote

At the end of PI Planning, every person votes (fist of five):
- **5** — Fully confident
- **4** — Mostly confident
- **3** — Some concerns
- **2** — Major concerns
- **1** — Cannot commit

Target: Average ≥ 3.5. If below, concerns must be resolved before committing.
