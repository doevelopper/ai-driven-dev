# Iteration Planning

Iteration Planning is a team ceremony held at the start of every iteration to select and plan the work for the next 2 weeks.

## Duration
- **2-week iteration**: ~4 hours (split across morning sessions as needed)

## Inputs
- Prioritized Team Backlog (groomed, ready stories)
- PI Objectives (what the team is committed to in this PI)
- Team capacity (accounting for leave, holidays, partial availability)
- Carry-over stories from previous iteration

## Agenda

| Segment | Duration | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Context | 15 min | PO reviews iteration goals and PI Objectives alignment |
| Story review | 60 min | Team reviews and accepts/splits stories |
| Task breakdown | 60 min | Team breaks stories into tasks (<1 day each) |
| Capacity check | 15 min | Confirm velocity vs. capacity |
| Commitment | 15 min | Team commits to iteration goals |

## Outputs
- Iteration Goal (2–3 sentences)
- Committed stories with tasks
- Iteration board updated
