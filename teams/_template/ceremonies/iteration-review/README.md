# Iteration Review (Team Demo)

The Iteration Review is a ceremony at the end of each iteration where the team demonstrates completed work to stakeholders and receives feedback.

## Duration
~1 hour per team

## Participants
- Development Team
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Business Owners (optional)
- Other stakeholders (optional)

## Agenda

| Segment | Duration | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 5 min | Scrum Master recaps iteration goals |
| Story demos | 30–40 min | Each story owner demos the working software |
| Feedback discussion | 10 min | Stakeholders provide feedback |
| Backlog adjustment | 5 min | PO adjusts backlog based on feedback |

## Definition of "Done" for Demo

A story is only demoed if:
- All acceptance criteria are met
- Automated tests pass in CI
- PO has accepted the story
