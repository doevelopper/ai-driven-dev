# Daily Stand-up

The Daily Stand-up (Daily Scrum) is a 15-minute synchronization event for the team to align on progress and surface blockers.

## Format

Each team member briefly shares:

1. **What did I complete since last stand-up?**
2. **What will I work on today?**
3. **Do I have any blockers or impediments?**

## Rules

- Time-boxed: **15 minutes maximum**
- Same time and place every day
- Scrum Master facilitates and captures impediments
- Detailed discussions happen **after** the stand-up
- Absent members post updates asynchronously

## Impediment Escalation

| Severity | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Team can self-resolve | Resolve during the day |
| Needs PO decision | PO addresses within 2 hours |
| Cross-team dependency | Raised in ART Sync by Scrum Master |
| Organizational blocker | Escalated to RTE |
