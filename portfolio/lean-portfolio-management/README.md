# Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)

LPM is a SAFe core competency that aligns strategy and execution through three primary responsibilities: **Strategy & Investment Funding**, **Agile Portfolio Operations**, and **Lean Governance**.

## Three Responsibilities

### 1. Strategy & Investment Funding
- Define and evolve Strategic Themes
- Establish Value Streams and funding guardrails
- Run Participatory Budgeting
- Maintain Portfolio Vision and Roadmap

### 2. Agile Portfolio Operations
- Coordinate ARTs and Solution Trains
- Manage Portfolio Kanban and epic flow
- Support PI execution across value streams
- Coach Lean-Agile practices

### 3. Lean Governance
- Forecast and budget dynamically
- Measure portfolio performance (OKRs, flow metrics)
- Ensure compliance and audit readiness
- Drive continuous improvement

## Sub-folders

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `value-streams/` | Value stream definitions and funding |
| `guardrails/` | Spending, WIP, and policy guardrails |
| `okrs/` | Portfolio-level OKRs |

## LPM Cadences

| Meeting | Frequency | Participants |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic Portfolio Review | Quarterly | Business Owners, Enterprise Architects, LPM |
| Portfolio Sync | Bi-weekly | Epic Owners, RTE, STE, LPM |
| Participatory Budgeting | Semi-annually | All stakeholders |
