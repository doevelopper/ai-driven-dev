# Strategic Themes

Strategic Themes are differentiating business objectives that connect a SAFe portfolio to the strategy of the enterprise. They provide context and guidance for portfolio decision-making.

## Purpose

- Guide portfolio investment and prioritization
- Align value streams to enterprise direction
- Inform Epic and Capability selection
- Drive OKR definition at all levels

## Structure of a Strategic Theme

```markdown
## Theme: <Name>

**Statement:** <What we aim to achieve and why it matters>

**Time horizon:** <This year / Next 2 years>

**Linked OKRs:**
- Objective: ...
  - KR1: ...
  - KR2: ...

**Linked Epics:** <!-- TODO: link to epics -->

**Owner:** <!-- TODO: Business Owner name -->
```
